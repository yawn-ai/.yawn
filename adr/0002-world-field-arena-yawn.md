# ADR 0002: World, field, arena, and Yawn are distinct

- Status: proposed
- Date: 2026-08-13
- Decision authority: founding maintainer acceptance required

## Context

“World,” “field,” “arena,” and “Yawn” had been used near one another without an
executable temporal or holarchy grammar. Treating them as aliases would make it
hard to state what the model cannot see and when a record should attach, nest,
link, merge, or split.

## Decision

World is the open horizon exceeding the model. Field is attributed available
information. Arena is a provisional relevance boundary. Yawn is a durable
orientation contract inside an arena. Turn is a causal episode through which
the contract may move or wait.

World Views, Arena Views, timelines, and filesystems are derived over
canonical IDs and events. They cannot determine truth, permission, or identity.

## Consequences

Arena discovery becomes the precondition for structural routing. Lacunae and
frame limits remain visible. The same agent can participate in several arenas,
and turns can overlap without forcing one global game clock.

## Alternatives

World-as-arena, arena-as-Yawn, folder-as-identity, and fixed turn alternation
were rejected because they erase partial access, contractual boundaries, or
causal concurrency.
