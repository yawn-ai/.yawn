Sophia asked Daddy: "Can I work on your computer? I want to learn more. I just wanna spell stuff so I can read." Those are her words. Kindergarten will check whether she can feel how many, so Daddy made this for her to do by herself, on the real computer. It shows one thing. It waits. It asks. She taps what she sees, and it says what it noticed. Nobody keeps score, and nothing can be lost. Daddy's guess, not her words: she likes to do it herself and wants the letters to mean something. If that guess is wrong, she gets to say so. It never ends; when she is ready, it adds a little more.
