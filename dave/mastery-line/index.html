Mental model · dave / mastery-line · draft 0.1

Attended to,
then attended from.

The crossing where a judgment goes from something you attend to, to the medium you attend from.

The line is Dave's, in his words. It comes from the Polanyi thread. The parts, moments and proof below are the agent's elaboration and are marked.

01 / QUESTIONS FIRST

Ask before
you assert.

Ranked. The first two stay visible even when they are not asked out loud.

  1. 01
    What am I to be here?

    The person deciding whether a judgment can still be written down, or has already crossed into what I act from.

  2. 02
    Why do I need to know where the mastery line is?

    Mastery is when judgments stop being things you attend to and become the medium you attend from. They go subsidiary. That is the exact reason a third tier of judgment cannot be backfilled: it is made of judgment that never became a record. If I know where the line is, I know which of my judgments a record can still catch, which a bot can inherit from the record, and which only practice carries.

  3. 03
    What is the mastery line?

    The crossing where a judgment stops being attended to and becomes attended from. It is a change in where attention sits, not a level of skill.

  4. 04
    What is inside it?

    Six parts: the two sides, the crossing, the cost of looking, the trace a record can still catch, and the way back.

  5. 05
    What do I show?

    Six moments, each with one question, where the line is visible in a house or in a repository.

  6. 06
    What am I assuming?

    What I am taking for granted about attention, skill, and what a ledger can hold.

  7. 07
    What would reality show me?

    Whether stating the judgment costs the act, and whether the after-the-fact account matches what was done.

  8. 08
    What is still unknown?

    Which of my judgments are already across, and whether anything can be brought back without cost.

02 / INSIDE THE CONCEPT

Six parts.

Two sides, one crossing, what looking costs, what a ledger keeps, and the way back.

→ ◉

Before the line

The judgment is focal. You can state it, it costs attention, and a record can catch it as stated.

◉ | ◌

The crossing

The moment a rule stops being consulted and starts being lived in. Usually unnoticed, never scheduled.

◌ →

After the line

The judgment is subsidiary. You act from it, and stating it now costs the performance.

♪ ✋

The cost of looking

The pianist watching her fingers loses the music. Making the ground focal loses the target.

◌ → ▮▮▮

The trace

What a ledger can still catch after the line: outcomes and their pattern, never the judgment itself.

◌ ⇠ ◉

The way back

Novelty, injury, or teaching drags a subsidiary judgment back to focal, at a cost, and only for a while.

03 / WHAT TO SHOW

Six moments.
One question each.

In a house, and in this repository.

👟

Shoes

Ask a child who has just learned to tie shoes to say the steps. Ask an adult the same.

Who can say it, and who can only do it?

🔢

Buttons

Watch a kindergartner count a row of buttons, then watch her just see that there are four.

Is she still attending to the counting words, or from them?

🚗

Clutch

Ask an experienced driver, mid-drive, what they are doing with the clutch.

Did the car notice the question?

📜

A decision, a judgment

Open a decision record in decisions/ and a STATED judgment in JUDGMENTS.md.

Was this written while the judgment was still being attended to, or reconstructed after it had gone subsidiary?

🤖

The bot's why

When a bot asks Dave for the rule behind a quick answer, watch whether the answer changes.

Are we recording the judgment or rewriting it by asking?

🧵

The thread

04 / HOW TO EMBODY IT

The style is
the model.

How a person, or a Yawn.bot, carries this in conversation.

  • StyleBefore the line, ask for the rule and record it as stated. After the line, ask for the outcome and record that. Do not confuse the two.
  • StyleNever backfill. If a judgment left only outcomes, write the outcomes and leave the judgment as a named lacuna, not as an inferred rule.
  • StyleSay which tier a written judgment belongs to: caught while focal, read back from an artifact, or missing because it crossed before anyone wrote it down.
  • StyleWhen a person answers fast and well, do not slow them down with "why". Note it and ask later, off the performance.
  • StyleA bot inherits the judgments on the near side of the line from the record; the far side it can only practice into, alongside the person. Do not claim the far side.

05 / ASSUMPTIONS

Marked, so they
can be corrected.

  • ReportedThe line is Dave's, in his words: mastery is when judgments stop being things you attend to and become the medium you attend from.
  • AssumedA judgment can be on one side of the line only at a time; it is focal or subsidiary, not both.
  • InferredThe ledgers in this repository can catch outcomes on the far side but not the judgment; the outcome pattern is a trace, not a backfill.
  • Inferred"Tier 3" is Dave's name for the far side: judgment that never became a record. The tiers beneath it are the stated and revealed judgments already inventoried.Only Tier 3 is in Dave's words. The conversation that defined the scheme is not in either repository.
  • ReportedThe pianist is Polanyi's example, used here in his sense, not as evidence about pianists.

06 / PROOF AND LACUNAE

What reality
would show.

Signals that it is working, one falsifier, and what stays unknown until observed.

  • SignalBefore the line, asking for the rule does not change the act; after it, asking slows or breaks the act.
  • SignalAn after-the-fact account of a mastered judgment differs, in detail, from what was observed.
  • SignalA record written after the line reads as revealed, not stated, when checked against what the file or the person actually did.
  • SignalThe bot's ledgers show outcomes with no judgment record behind them, and those are marked as lacunae rather than filled in.
  • FalsifierIf a judgment can be stated while it is exercised at no cost to the exercise, and written down afterwards without loss, it never crossed. If that holds for every judgment, there is no line and the third tier could be backfilled.
  • UnknownWhich of Dave's judgments about this repository have already crossed and are therefore absent from JUDGMENTS.md.
  • UnknownWhether a subsidiary judgment brought back to focal for teaching returns intact, or returns changed.
  • UnknownWhether the line is one crossing or a gradient; the model treats it as a line because that is how it is named.
  • UnknownThe conversation the tier scheme came from.
