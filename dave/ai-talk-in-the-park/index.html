01 / QUESTIONS FIRST

The scope
on duty.

Ranked. The role is the game that is running for one hour; everything else is inside it.

    What am I to be here?

    Speaker Dave. The scope on duty for one hour on the grass: transfer presence, say only what I would say to a neighbour, and hand parents the chain.

  2. 02
    Why do I need to know this?

    Because my kids are four and five and the world they are walking into is one nobody can see. The talk is how I find the other parents who feel that, and how I practice saying it plainly enough that a five-year-old could follow.

  3. 03
    What is it?

    A free, quiet hour in a Parker park where I talk about raising small kids into a world where AI is arriving, and I start with a yawn.

  4. 04
    What is inside it?

    The yawn as the shared, unexplained thing; the chain a child can carry; the game you are playing right now; who is speaking; where and when; what my bot does and does not do.

  5. 05
    What do I show?

    A blanket. The first yawn, mine. One question at a time.

  6. 06

    That presence transfers in an hour; that the Town's permit path is as reported; that parents are the audience; that the bot's candidate dates are close enough to start from.
