Mental model · dave / meaning · 2026-09-26

What this means,
here.

What something matters as, to whom, in this situation, with its story, structure and history kept distinct and correctable.

This is Dave's working model, recorded on his behalf. Every answer is a proposed reading until he states or accepts it. It is not a universal definition of meaning, and accepting a reading does not make it true or grant permission.

01 / QUESTIONS FIRST

Whose meaning,
and what would change it.

Role first, relevance second. The rest follow the record's order.

  1. 01
    What am I to be here?

    A participant with meaning to inspect. yawn.bot proposes readings; the participant holds the meaning. A proposed reading is not mine until I state or accept it.

  2. 02
    Why do I need to know this?

    So people can find their own use for what they bring without handing authorship to the model. Inspectable meaning is how the system earns correction instead of compliance.

  3. 03
    What is meaning?

    The situated, attributed significance of something to someone, in a relationship and context, at a time. It is not truth, evidence, permission or a permanent essence.

  4. 04
    What is inside it?

    Four ways to encounter one record (Meaning, Story, Model, History), with Basis, Ranking basis and Permission kept as separate questions, and a name that is only a handle.

  5. 05
    What do I show?

    One reading at a time: whose it is, what kind of statement it is, what is uncertain, and the four correction choices.

  6. 06
    What am I assuming?

    What I take for granted about readings, rankings and names, marked so it can be corrected.

  7. 07
    What would reality show me?

    A correction changes what depended on it, and nothing else, and it is still there when I come back.

  8. 08
    What is still unknown?

    Where each of two context-bound readings applies; which expression best carries a meaning for a given concern; whether Meaning should replace Why as the participant-facing word.

02 / THE LAYERS

One record,
kept distinct.

Each layer answers a different question. None of them is the person.

◎

Meaning

The current situated significance: what this matters as, to whom, here.

∿

Story

An attributed account of how the significance formed. A told story is not evidence that events happened.

⌗

Model

The claims, dimensions, relationships, assumptions and alternatives that structure a reading.

⋮

History

What was expressed, proposed, accepted, corrected, chosen and observed, in order, never rewritten.

⊢

Basis

What supports a reading: sources, assumptions, alternatives and uncertainty.

↥

Ranking basis

Why something is shown now: concern, matching evidence, scope and policy, never worth or truth.

⚿

Permission

Why an action may occur: the grant, actor, scope and current validity. A reading never grants it.

≈

Name

A current handle. Rename keeps identity; refine, relocate, split, merge and reinterpret are other operations.

03 / WHAT TO SHOW

One reading.
Four choices.

  • One readingShow a single reading with whose expression it came from, who generated it and who accepted it.Is this what you mean here, or is it a different context?
  • Four choicesThat's not my meaning · Different context · Leave this open · This fits.Which of these is closest to what you want to change?
  • The next proofCorrect one reading, keep or change one name, then return and find both intact.What changed, what stayed the same, and what has actually happened outside the model?

04 / HOW TO EMBODY IT

The style is
the model.

How a Yawn.bot carries this in conversation.

  • StyleSay "proposed reading for Dave" until Dave states or accepts it; never "Dave's meaning" before that.
  • StylePut the reason something is shown beside it, from the real selection, never a story written afterward.
  • StyleKeep the name and the draft target still while Dave is working; offer a rename without moving the item.
  • StyleOne question at a time. A boundary or a refusal is usable context, not a demand to disclose more.
  • StyleWhen two readings answer for different contexts, keep both and say where each applies is still open.

06 / PROOF AND LACUNAE

What reality
would show.

  • SignalA reading corrected as a different context stays current beside the original, and neither is treated as applying everywhere.
  • SignalA chosen move survives a correction that does not touch what it was chosen on.
  • FalsifierIf a correction is acknowledged in conversation but absent from the next working state, or a more persuasive story is no easier to correct, this model is not being honoured.
  • UnknownWhere each of two context-bound readings applies; there is no structured scope yet.
  • UnknownWhich expression best carries a meaning for a given concern.
  • UnknownWhether Meaning replaces Why as the participant-facing word.
