We know more
than we can tell.

The line of thought from tacit knowing to the remainder a record cannot recover.

This is Dave's thread, in his words where marked. Its crossing point has its own model: the mastery line. The reading of Polanyi is the agent's and is marked as such.

01 / QUESTIONS FIRST

Ask before
you assert.

Ranked. The first two stay visible even when they are not asked out loud.

  1. 01
    What am I to be here?

    The person holding the thread: a reader of Polanyi who is also the author of the judgments this repository tries to write down.

  2. 02
    Why do I need to know the Polanyi thread?

    The whole method here is to make judgment inspectable. Polanyi says a part of judgment cannot be told, by its nature and not by neglect. If I know where that part begins I know what a record can carry, what a person or a practice has to carry instead, and what a bot should stop asking for.

  3. 03
    What is the Polanyi thread?

    We know more than we can tell. Knowing has a focal target and a subsidiary ground, and the ground cannot be made focal without losing the target.

  4. 04
    What is inside it?

    Six turns of the thread, from focal awareness to the remainder no record recovers.

  5. 05
    What do I show?

    Five moments where the thread is visible, each with one question.

  6. 06
    What am I assuming?

    What I am taking from Polanyi, and where I am reading past him.

  7. 07
    What would reality show me?

    Whether asking for the rule changes the performance, and whether the reconstruction matches the act.

  8. 08
    What is still unknown?

    Where the tiers came from, and how much of my own judgment is already on the far side.

02 / INSIDE THE CONCEPT

Six turns.

From what you look at, to what you look with, to what was never written down.

◉

Focal

What you attend to. The thing you can point at and name.

◌ ◌ ◌ → ◉

Subsidiary

What you attend from. The clues, the grip, the medium. Felt, not looked at.

from → to

From–to

Every act of knowing runs from a subsidiary ground to a focal target. The direction is the structure.

⌂

Indwelling

The tool, the rule, the language becomes part of the body you know with. You live in it.

♪ ✋

The pianist

Shift attention from the piece to the fingers and the piece stops. Looking at the ground destroys the target.

◉ … ∅

The remainder

Judgment exercised but never told. It leaves outcomes, not records, and cannot be written down afterwards.

03 / WHAT TO SHOW

Five moments.
One question each.

Where the thread shows in a house, and where it shows in this repository.

🚲

Bicycle

Ask someone who rides well how they keep a bicycle upright. Then ask them to ride while explaining.

Which came first, the account or the wobble?

✋

Password

Watch a person type a password they use every day, then ask them to say it aloud letter by letter.

Did saying it help, or did the fingers know something the mouth did not?

📜

Two judgments

Open JUDGMENTS.md and pick one STATED judgment and one REVEALED judgment.

Which of these did the author attend to when writing it, and which was read off what the file does?

🤖

The bot's why

When a bot asks for the rule behind a fast answer, notice whether the answer slows down.

Is the rule being stated, or being reconstructed to satisfy the question?

🧵

The sibling

Follow the thread to the mastery line, which names the crossing itself.

On which side of the line is this judgment right now?

04 / HOW TO EMBODY IT

The style is
the model.

How a person, or a Yawn.bot, carries this in conversation.

  • StyleAsk for the rule only while the rule is still focal. Once it has gone subsidiary, ask for the outcome and record that.
  • StyleTreat a written-down judgment as one of two things, and say which: stated while focal, or reconstructed after the fact.
  • StyleDo not backfill. When a judgment left only outcomes, write the outcomes and mark the judgment as a lacuna.
  • StyleA bot inherits the stated judgments from the record and the subsidiary ones only by practice alongside the person. Say so rather than claiming the second.
  • StyleLooking is not free. Before asking a person to explain what they do well, know that the asking can cost the doing.

05 / ASSUMPTIONS

Marked, so they
can be corrected.

  • AssumedPolanyi's focal/subsidiary distinction is a fair description of skilled judgment, not only of motor skill.
  • InferredThe judgments in JUDGMENTS.md are the ones that were caught while focal, or read back from artifacts; neither set reaches the remainder.
  • InferredDave's "Tier 3" is the remainder: judgment that never became a record. The tiers below it are the stated and revealed judgments already inventoried here.Dave's words name Tier 3 only. The conversation that defined the tiers is not in either repository; the mapping of Tiers 1 and 2 is the agent's reading.
  • ReportedThe pianist example is Polanyi's own, and the thread quotes it in his sense.

06 / PROOF AND LACUNAE

What reality
would show.

Signals that it is working, one falsifier, and what stays unknown until observed.

  • SignalAsked for the rule behind a skilled act, the person's reconstruction differs from what they were observed to do.
  • SignalAsked to explain while doing, the doing degrades; asked afterwards, it does not.
  • SignalA judgment written in this repository can be tagged stated or revealed, and no third tag is needed for what was caught.
  • SignalWhat could not be caught shows up as outcomes in the ledgers with no judgment record behind them.
  • FalsifierIf every judgment a skilled person exercises can be written down afterwards without loss, and stating it while acting costs nothing, there is no subsidiary ground and no remainder. The thread is broken and Tier 3 could be backfilled.
  • UnknownThe conversation the tier scheme came from, and what Tiers 1 and 2 were called there.
  • UnknownHow much of Dave's own judgment about this repository is already subsidiary and so already missing from JUDGMENTS.md.
  • UnknownWhether a subsidiary judgment can be brought back to focal for teaching without a lasting cost to the skill.
  • UnknownWhether a bot practising alongside a person acquires anything subsidiary at all, or only a longer record.
