# Support

For usage questions, unclear documentation, or interoperability ideas, open a
[GitHub issue](https://github.com/yawn-ai/.yawn/issues/new) with the smallest
example that preserves the problem.

For product feedback, use the [feedback route](https://yawn-ai.github.io/.yawn/feedback/).

For vulnerabilities, credentials, private records, or sensitive conduct
reports, do not use a public issue. Follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) or
[CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).

Support is best-effort. This experimental protocol is not a substitute for
professional legal, medical, financial, or crisis support.
