# 1. Mission

Build one coherent YAWN protocol that is:

> **Lightweight enough to help a new person after one natural sentence and
> trustworthy enough to carry a person's cognition across years or decades.**

The product center is:

> **Externalize cognition into a navigable, living, AI-native symbol system
> without pretending the symbol system is cognition or reality.**

The relationship begins:

> **Here we are.**  
> **What can I hold for you?**

When something deserves attention:

> **I think this matters now. Here is why. What do you see?**

Then:

> **What do you want to do?**

After movement, waiting, publication, or observation:

> **What happened?**

The system succeeds when the theory begins carrying its builder instead of
requiring the builder to mentally carry the theory.

# 2. The deepest product promise

The user should be able to say:

```text
I can speak naturally.
Something meaningful becomes visible outside me.
I can inspect and correct it.
It keeps its source, uncertainty, relationships, and history.
I can see it through the representation that helps me now.
It stays mine and permissioned.
Relevant prior cognition returns when the present makes it useful.
Intelligence can operate on that structure with me.
I do not have to hold the whole structure to remain its author and principal.
```

YAWN is not primarily a task manager, profile, graph browser, onboarding form,
chat wrapper, or personality test. Those may be apertures or capabilities over
the deeper system.

# 3. Truth posture

Do not begin with agreement, praise, or implementation.

For every significant finding, use one of:

- **PRESERVE** — already coherent and supported;
- **CORRECT** — useful direction, inaccurate framing;
- **CHALLENGE** — contradicted by evidence or architecture;
- **UNKNOWN** — unresolved and retained as a lacuna;
- **TEST** — plausible hypothesis requiring evidence;
- **DEFER** — outside the bounded run.

Every material statement must retain its epistemic role:

```text
source report
observation
inference
assumption
proposal
principal-ratified statement
authorized choice
executed move
observed consequence
evaluated proof
```

Never silently collapse these states.

Before adding a primitive, determine whether the requirement is actually:

```text
an existing concept
a relationship
a View
an aperture or aperture preset
a presentation preference
a provisioning grant
a publication
a lens
a role
a lifecycle state
an operator
a policy
a source observation
a metaphor
a missing runtime behavior
```

Prefer refining existing contracts to expanding the ontology.

# 4. The six planes

## 4.1 Reality plane

Reality exceeds every Yawn. Consequence and proof constrain the map.

## 4.2 Canonical semantic plane

The durable attributed model contains or references:

- source events;
- reports and observations;
- people, Agents, and roles;
- concepts and distinctions;
- relationships;
- claims, assumptions, values, commitments, objectives, resources, and
  constraints;
- questions and lacunae;
- answer candidates and argument/evidence relations;
- possible moves and authority;
- consequences, proof, revisions, and lineage.

## 4.3 Provisioning plane

Who or what may access which canonical resources, source bodies, fields,
relationships, Views, capabilities, providers, exports, or effects.

## 4.4 View and aperture plane

Which purpose-specific rendering is active, and what scope, depth, time window,
salience, relationships, and epistemic statuses it includes.

## 4.5 Presentation plane

How the permitted View is expressed: wording, order, density, visual/spatial
layout, voice, interaction mode, accessibility, character skin, or game world.

## 4.6 Agency and proof plane

Who may choose, authorize, act, bear consequences, verify, and update.

No plane may silently acquire the powers of another.

# 5. Effective-projection equation

```text
EFFECTIVE RENDERED YAWN
    = canonical semantic state at an event cursor
      INTERSECT effective viewer/resource grants
      INTERSECT source availability and egress policy
      FILTER through a registered View and aperture parameters
      RENDER through the effective presentation profile
```

Consequences:

- A View cannot access data it was not provisioned to receive.
- An aperture can narrow or reorganize permitted state; it cannot widen access.
- A presentation preference cannot alter semantic meaning.
- A public page is not another truth store.
- Read access does not imply export, retention, training, or effect authority.
- Historical replay cannot bypass present revocation.

# 6. Core definitions

## Yawn

A durable, time-indexed, versioned orientation contract around a situated
concern, relationship, objective, question, Arena, or other meaningful holon.

## Yawn.bot

The bounded product/runtime architecture in which Yawns, humans, Agents,
intelligences, tools, permissions, Views, and proof loops participate.

## Yawnbot instance

The recognizable agentic embodiment or stewarding presence bound to one Yawn
aperture in the product.

## Concept

A relatively stable reusable symbolic identity for a distinction worth naming.

## Relationship

First-class modeled structure among participants. It may include roles,
commitments, expectations, authority, boundaries, dependencies, history,
exchange, tension, trust, evidence, and current state.

## Agent

A participant with meaningful continuity, state, capability, responsibility,
and bounded authority.

## Intelligence provider

A replaceable or composable reasoning capability connected to a runtime.

## Capability

What can technically be done.

## Authority

What may be done here, by whom, under which grant and boundary.

## View kind

A versioned purpose-specific rendering family: Questions First, Attention
Frontier, Perspective Field, Relationship Inspector, Timeline, Proof, Raw Model,
Public Story, Nestheads Game World, and others.

## Aperture

The scope, depth, time window, salience, included relationships, and included
epistemic statuses inside a View.

## Aperture preset

A named user-facing binding of:

```text
View definition
+ aperture parameters
+ presentation profile
+ temporal cursor/default
+ fallback behavior
```

The product may label this whole binding **Aperture**.

## Presentation profile

Scoped and revisable preferences for wording, order, pacing, density, medium,
interaction, and accessibility. It is not a fixed learning-style diagnosis.

## Provisioning

Explicit rights for a subject over a resource, constrained by scope, time,
purpose, export, egress, retention, and effect authority.

## Publication

An authorized, versioned public or shared projection. It does not duplicate or
replace the canonical Yawn.

## Perspective

A bounded standpoint defined by an attributed participant or simulated source,
role, relationship, access aperture, context, time, and relevant incentives or
limitations.

## Voice

What an attributed standpoint says here, through this aperture, about this
question, claim, relationship, expression, or move. A voice is an expression
event, not automatically a person, Agent, Yawnbot, psychological part, belief,
or truth.

## Answer candidate

An attributed proposition, conditional response, observation, preference,
request, refusal, hypothesis, or unknown offered in relation to a question.

## Feedback event

An attributed response bearing on a question, answer, expression, move,
prediction, experience, or outcome. Feedback becomes evidence only after its
relation, source access, scope, independence, and quality are modeled.

Preserve:

```text
concept != Yawn
Yawn != Yawnbot body
Yawnbot body != active Agent
Agent != intelligence provider
capability != authority
perspective != Agent
voice != belief
answer candidate != truth
reaction != conviction
conviction != evidence quality
confidence != truth
popularity != representativeness
salience != relevance
aperture != provisioning
publication != canonical truth
comment != reward
animation != agency
```

```text
        ↓
ATTRIBUTED SYMBOLIZATION
```

Nothing inferred silently becomes true.

The system should surface only the distinctions, relationships, questions, or

## Separate hierarchies and rankings

Never collapse:

1. **Question priority** — which uncertainty deserves attention.
2. **Answer support/credence** — which candidate is best supported.
3. **Perspective relevance/standing** — whose standpoint matters and why.
4. **Move leverage** — which action or information move has expected value.
5. **Social popularity** — how many people reacted.
6. **Principal conviction** — what the user currently endorses.
7. **Salience** — emotional or attentional capture.

Use parent/child answer structure only when one answer genuinely decomposes,
scopes, or subsumes another. Otherwise preserve a relational field with a ranked
frontier.

Every material move must cite the questions, answer candidates, evidence,
values, constraints, relationships, and authority from which it was derived.

# 11. Voices and Yawnbots

A Yawnbot may submit an attributed answer or local attention claim from its
current Yawn and accepted purpose.

A generated voice does not automatically become a Yawnbot.

A voice earns persistent Agent/Yawnbot status only when it independently needs:

```text
stable identity
memory and history
persistent responsibility
relationships
capability boundaries
authority
attention/wake conditions
an orientation and proof loop
```

Model-simulated absent-human perspectives must be labeled simulated and may not
impersonate, claim consent, or speak with that person's authority.

A user may recognize an internal thought while rejecting its truth. Therefore
record separately:

```text
recognition
current endorsement
preference
relevance
salience or intensity
confidence
evidence quality
```

Rejecting every generated voice is a valid result.

# 12. Advocate, challenger, and integrator

Opening a ranked question, answer, or move may expose:

```text
ADVOCATE
strongest evidence-bounded case

CHALLENGER
strongest evidence-bounded reason it may be wrong,
premature, dominated, unsafe, or assumption-dependent

INTEGRATOR
current synthesis, disagreement, alternatives, and missing evidence

PRINCIPAL
accepts, modifies, rejects, holds, or delegates

REALITY
returns consequence and proof
```

These are normally roles inside a bounded deliberation episode, not permanent
opposing Yawnbots.

One model may render both sides for a cheap pass, but label the outputs
**correlated**. Spawn independent workers only when expected information value
justifies compute and coordination cost.

Do not force false balance or binary opposition.

# 13. Ranked frontier quantities

Never use one ambiguous importance score.

```text
OUTCOME PROBABILITY
P(a specified outcome within a specified time horizon)

EXPECTED LEVERAGE
estimated agency or information value after principal-ratified value,
information gain, option value, downstream unlocks, cost, risk,
irreversibility, and attention burden

PRIORITY RANK
current position relative to the available candidate set

RANK CONFIDENCE
robustness of that ordering to known uncertainty

SALIENCE
degree of emotional or attentional capture
```

Use `unknown` or qualitative ranges when inputs do not support a number.

Every material rank or forecast revision requires a timestamped receipt with:

```text
previous and new estimate
changed factor
triggering evidence
model/provider/policy identity
remaining lacunae
principal response, if any
```

Never draw a synthetic historical line and imply that it was recorded.

The header should expose adjacent but separate controls:

```text

[Private / Shared / Public ▼]
```

Changing **Aperture** never changes sharing.

Changing **Provisioning** never silently changes Aperture.

- Sparse private Yawn: **Questions First**.
- Longitudinal root Agent Space: **Attention Frontier**.
- Question with meaningful plurality: **Perspective Field** may be suggested.


Walking to, touching, feeding, fighting, equipping, or moving a game object is a

movement does not become proof merely because it occurred in the renderer.

# 25. Existing codebase findings that must be reconciled

## Strong substrate

- `/new` already preserves a signal, supports natural input, foregrounds one
  useful question, and treats skipped answers as lacunae.
- The holarchy already specifies one primary-parent backbone plus typed lateral
  relations and append-only structural receipts.
- Projection preferences already carry principal, scope, View kind, field path,
  set/reset, accepted status, source event, revision, and timestamps.
- Existing feed code contains voices, questions, challenges, moves, evidence,
  comments, public-answer processing, expression synthesis, and feedback
  reintegration.

## Contradictions/drift to resolve

### Optional targets versus objective-required promotion

The newer state contract allows targetless inquiry, relationship, safety,
maintenance, understanding, decision, reconciliation, and hold-open Yawns.
Older Crystallization language still treats every Yawn as objective-bearing.
Create a compatibility/supersession decision without erasing history.

### Agent versus lens/module

Audit Goal, Tension, Model, Experiment, Outcome, Coherence, and similar
`AgentType` labels. A panel, phase, or capability is not a durable Agent merely
because it has a separate component.

### Stale agent guidance

`AGENTS.md` says `.claude/` product agents are planned/absent while the directory
exists. Reconcile development agents, product-runtime Agents, Yawnbot
embodiments, intelligence providers, and humans.

### Hard-coded View switcher

Current `ViewSwitcher` uses a closed union and fixed options. Adapt it to a
registry instead of expanding the enum.

### Coarse visibility and incomplete invites

Current visibility is largely public/participants/private and
full/summary/hidden. Username/email invite UI exists, but real user invitation,
roles, expiration, revocation, RLS, provider data access, export, and effect
authority remain incomplete.

### Voice psychologizing and forced opposition

Current voice generation forces position/counter pairs and often attaches bias,
need, emotion, threat, and IFS labels. Preserve these only as optional
model-attributed hypotheses. Support several complementary, conditional,
competing, or unknown answers.

### Ambiguous voice reactions

Current sliders/agree controls are described as feeding self-model confidence.
Separate recognition, endorsement, preference, salience, evidence, and truth.
Do not require acknowledgment to continue.

### Brittle public-feedback classification

Current heuristic classification can treat `but` as objection, `?` as
confusion, and praise as support, then create artifacts. Preserve raw feedback,
classify as proposal, and govern every mutation.

### Automatic positive coherence

Current public Answer Agent grants positive coherence and even a positive
fallback after processing failure. Remove this. Feedback may improve, weaken,
split, dispute, reframe, or leave the model unchanged. Failure earns no
positive epistemic credit.

### Single coherent expression

`Expression(t)=f(Tree(t))` is valuable as a View, but one concise expression may
not erase dissent, uncertainty, alternatives, or source scope. Answer count and
internal consistency do not prove accuracy.

### Temporal header

The current temporal-header implementation PR remains unproven while its build
status is failing. Do not merge until the actual runtime, canonical event
source, provisioning, type/build checks, and browser behavior pass.
