# Code of conduct

YAWN is about making perspectives, uncertainty, boundaries, and authority more
legible. The contribution space should practice the same standard.

## Expected conduct

- Treat people as people, not objects to classify or optimize.
- Critique claims, schemas, and consequences without demeaning their authors.
- Name uncertainty and conflicts of interest.
- Respect privacy, consent, attribution, and requests to remove personal data.
- Make room for different levels of technical and philosophical fluency.
- Receive correction without retaliation and repair harm where possible.
- Do not use project language to diagnose, manipulate, threaten, or claim hidden
  authority over another person.

## Unacceptable conduct

Harassment, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, doxxing, intimidation,
sustained disruption, impersonation, undisclosed conflicts that affect review,
or publishing another person's sensitive information without permission are not
accepted in project spaces.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, project
events, and public representation of the project. Maintainers may edit or
remove content, restrict participation, or ban an account when necessary to
protect the community. Enforcement decisions should preserve evidence, explain
the applicable boundary when safe, and avoid exposing private reports.

Report conduct concerns to `yawn@yawn.ai`. For security vulnerabilities or
sensitive data exposure, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Reports will be
handled as privately as practical. Retaliation against a good-faith reporter is
itself a violation.

This is a living governance document. Material revisions are recorded in the
[changelog](CHANGELOG.md).
