# Competency questions

Status: **Normative test surface for working drafts**

The ontology earns its complexity by answering concrete questions without
inventing identity, authorship, intention, consent, causation, or authority.
Each executable contract should state which questions it answers and include a
fixture that proves the answer can be reconstructed.

## Situated observation

1. What became available?
2. Which Agent acquired it?
3. In which Arena and at what time?
4. Through which source or acquisition method?
5. Under which conditions and known limits?
6. Which later statements were grounded in that Observation?

## Attribution and interpretation

7. Who asserted each statement?
8. Is it observed, reported, inferred, assumed, predicted, disputed, or
   unknown?
9. Which exact source spans support it?
10. Did an AI inference remain attributed to the model run rather than the
    cited human?
11. Which subjects does the statement concern?
12. What remains contradictory or unresolved?

## Orientation and motivation

13. What working model is currently held, by whom, and at which revision?
14. Which Value, Goal, Target, or Lacuna is relevant?
15. Is a Target absent, proposed, ratified, rejected, or revoked?
16. Which choice selected the current Intention?
17. Can the Yawn remain valid when no Intention or Target is endorsed?

## Intention, Projection, and action

18. What did the Agent mean to understand, communicate, preserve, test,
    repair, change, or do?
19. What did the Agent actually put into the Arena?
20. How did that Projection represent—or fail to represent—the Intention?
21. Was the Projection an expression, claim, model, question, communication,
    proposal, commitment, or Move?
22. If it was a Move, which Authority Grant applied?
23. Which boundaries, privacy rules, and egress constraints applied?

## Consequence, proof, and replay

24. What happened after the Projection?
25. Was the Consequence expected, unexpected, mixed, unknown, or disputed?
26. Which facts are occurrences and which are causal interpretations?
27. What prediction, verifier, and falsifier evaluated the result?
28. Did the Move avoid declaring its own success?
29. Which authorized event updated state?
30. Does replay produce the recorded materialized-state hash?

## Multi-agent and holonic integrity

31. What did each Agent observe separately?
32. How did one Agent's Projection become another Agent's Observation?
33. Which interpretations differ without being overwritten?
34. Is a child Yawn independently inspectable and closable?
35. Is child authority a subset of parent authority plus an explicit grant?
36. Are lateral typed relations distinct from primary parenthood?
37. Can a View change without changing canonical semantic identity?

## Public and privacy-safe views

38. Does every public item carry its own disclosure basis?
39. Are private source bodies excluded?
40. Does the public View retain source metadata, hashes, and redaction receipts?
41. Do the page, download, QR route, YAWN.bot overlay, and YAWN.ai view report
    the same semantic snapshot hash?
42. Does a relationship remain an offer until every required principal
    explicitly consents?

## Admission rule

A new term or field should not enter the upper ontology unless at least one
competency question cannot be answered safely without it. See
[`term-admission.md`](term-admission.md).
